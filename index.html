🧠 About Me

Who I Am

I'm Jay — a grad student, an AI engineer, and someone who gets genuinely excited when a piece of software makes someone's life a little easier or a business decision a little smarter. I grew up in India, earned my undergrad at VIT, and moved to Boston to pursue my Master's at Northeastern University — drawn by the city, the research culture, and the chance to work on problems that actually matter.

These days I spend most of my time at the Institute for Experiential AI, where I get to work on real client problems — the kind where the data is messy, the stakes are real, and a working solution takes more than a clever idea. I love that gap between "this looks good in theory" and "this is running in production" — and I've spent the last few years learning how to close it.

I've also had the chance to publish research at Springer and IEEE, which taught me how to think rigorously about problems, not just solve them quickly. Outside of work, I'm curious about most things — how businesses make decisions, why some products feel magical and others don't, and whatever rabbit hole I fell into this week. I'm always open to a good conversation or an interesting problem.
